🌍 Culture et Civilisation: Les Pays Francophones(French Speaking Countries)

Map of French-speaking countries around the world

French is an official language in 29 countries around the world! These countries are spread across different regions:

  • 🌍 Europe (like France, Belgium, Switzerland)
  • 🌍 Africa (largest number of French-speaking countries)
  • 🌍 North America (Canada)
  • 🌍 Caribbean & Pacific islands (Haiti, French Polynesia)
