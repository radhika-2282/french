🎵 Activity: Practice Introductions

Practice introducing yourself and others!

🎭 Roleplay: À la papeterie (At the stationery shop)

🏪 ✏️ 🎒 💶

📍 Situation: A student visits a stationery shop to buy some pens and pencils.

👨‍💼
Vendeur / Vendeuse Shopkeeper
🧑‍🎓
Client(e) Customer
V
Vendeur: Bonjour !
Hello! / Good morning!
Bonjour, monsieur ! :Client
Hello, sir!
C
V
Vendeur: Vous désirez ?
May I help you? / What would you like?
Je voudrais deux stylos et trois crayons, s’il vous plaît. :Client
I would like two pens and three pencils, please.
C
V
Vendeur: Voilà ! Autre chose ?
Here you go! Anything else?
Oui, une gomme, s’il vous plaît. :Client
Yes, an eraser, please.
C
V
Vendeur: Très bien. Voilà.
Very good / Very well. Here it is.
C’est combien ? :Client
How much is it?
C
V
Vendeur: Ça fait cinq euros.
That is / That makes five euros.
Voilà, monsieur. :Client
Here you go, sir.
C
V
Vendeur: Merci beaucoup !
Thank you very much!
Merci. Au revoir ! :Client
Thank you. Goodbye!
C
V
Vendeur: Au revoir ! Bonne journée !
Goodbye! Have a nice day!

💡 Mots clés & Expressions utiles (Key Words & Phrases)

🖊️ un stylo = a pen
✏️ un crayon = a pencil
🧽 une gomme = an eraser
❓ Vous désirez ? = May I help you?
🛍️ Je voudrais... = I would like...
💶 C’est combien ? = How much is it?
➕ Autre chose ? = Anything else?
☀️ Bonne journée ! = Have a nice day!
