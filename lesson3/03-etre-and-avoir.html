💡 Focus on "On"

What it means:

  • On = Nous (We)
  • On = we / one / people

How to conjugate:

Even though it often means "we", we conjugate it exactly like il or elle!

  • Il a = he has
  • Elle a = she has
  • On a = we have / one has

💡 Focus: Un, Une, Des

Un - a/an or one (masculine singular).

Une - a/an or one (feminine singular).

Des - some (plural, masculine or feminine).
