🌍 Culture et Civilisation : L’Identité et les Symboles de la France

(French Identity and Symbols)

Fun Fact: France is the most visited country in the world, with over 90 million tourists every year! 🏰🥖

🛑

L'Hexagone

France is often called The Hexagon because of its shape! It is the largest country in the European Union.

💶

L'Euro (€)

France uses the Euro. Before 2002, they used Francs, which were used for over 600 years!

🐓

Le Coq

The proud Rooster is the national symbol. It represents the bravery and heritage of the French people!

🎵

La Marseillaise

The national anthem is La Marseillaise. It was written in 1792 and is a song about freedom and courage!

🤝

La Devise

The motto "Liberté, Égalité, Fraternité" was born during the French Revolution over 200 years ago!

🧀

Les Fromages

France makes over 1,600 types of cheese! Some people say there's a different cheese for every day of the year.

🥐🥖

Les Pâtisseries et Le Pain

The Baguette is so important that its recipe is protected by law! French people buy it fresh every morning.

🐌

Les Escargots

Snails are a famous delicacy. French people eat around 30,000 tons of snails every single year!

🍷

Le Vin

France is one of the world's top wine producers. Wine making has been part of French culture for over 2,000 years!

👗✨

La Mode & Les Parfums

Paris is the fashion capital! They make beautiful clothes and the best smelling perfumes!

🚴‍♂️

Le Tour de France

The most prestigious bicycle race in the world! It lasts 3 weeks and travels across mountains and through tiny villages.

🇫🇷

Le Drapeau Français

The French flag is called the Tricolore. Its colors are Blue, White, and Red (Bleu, Blanc, Rouge)!

🎬✨

Le Festival de Cannes

One of the most famous film festivals in the world! Movie stars from all over come to the French Riviera to celebrate cinema.

😘

La Bise

French people often greet friends by kissing on the cheeks! This is called "La Bise". The number of kisses changes depending on the city!
